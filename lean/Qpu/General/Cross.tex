\paragraph{\texttt{all\_complete}}
\[ \mathrm{faces} = \mathrm{coins} \cdot \mathrm{rays} \land \mathrm{faces} = \mathrm{rays} + \mathrm{rays} \land \mathrm{coil} = \mathrm{faces} \land \mathrm{amplitudes} = \mathrm{mint}\ \mathrm{bits} \land \mathrm{fused} = \mathrm{faces} \cdot \mathrm{mint} (\mathrm{bits} + \mathrm{seed}) \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/General/Cross.lean\#L20}

\paragraph{\texttt{amplitudes\_from\_sum}}
AMPLITUDE ASYMMETRY: asymmetric and symmetric forms of amplitude constraint.
\[ 2 \cdot \mathrm{amplitudes} = \mathrm{mint} (\mathrm{bits} + \mathrm{seed}) \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/General/Cross.lean\#L15}

\paragraph{\texttt{bits\_both\_forms}}
\[ \mathrm{bits} = \mathrm{vertices} \cdot \mathrm{hexbit} \land \mathrm{bits} = \mathrm{mint} (n + \mathrm{coins}) \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/General/Cross.lean\#L13}

\paragraph{\texttt{bits\_exponential}}
COMPLETE CUBIC PROOF: bits as product and as nested exponential (via mint).
\[ \mathrm{bits} = \mathrm{mint} (n + \mathrm{coins}) \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/General/Cross.lean\#L12}

\paragraph{\texttt{coins\_bridges\_forms}}
\[ \mathrm{coins} = 2 \land \forall (x : \mathbb{N}), \mathrm{coins} \cdot x = x + x \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/General/Cross.lean\#L9}

\paragraph{\texttt{double\_is\_sum}}
ASYMMETRIC ↔ SYMMETRIC BRIDGES: coins=2 enables transformation between product and sum forms.
\[ \forall (x : \mathbb{N}), x + x = 2 \cdot x \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/General/Cross.lean\#L8}

\paragraph{\texttt{fused\_additive\_form}}
FUSED COMPLETENESS: quantum defines multiplicatively; can be distributed additively.
\[ \mathrm{fused} + \mathrm{fused} = \mathrm{faces} \cdot \mathrm{mint} (\mathrm{bits} + \mathrm{coins}) \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/General/Cross.lean\#L17}

\paragraph{\texttt{fused\_both\_directions}}
\[ \mathrm{fused} = \mathrm{faces} \cdot \mathrm{mint} (\mathrm{bits} + \mathrm{seed}) \land \mathrm{fused} + \mathrm{fused} = \mathrm{faces} \cdot \mathrm{mint} (\mathrm{bits} + \mathrm{coins}) \]
