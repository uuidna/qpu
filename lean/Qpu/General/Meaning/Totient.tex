\paragraph{\texttt{totient\_eq\_nat\_totient}}
Euler's totient function. This counts the number of naturals strictly less than `n` which are coprime with `n`.
\[ \forall (n : \mathbb{N}), \mathrm{totient}\ n = \mathrm{n.totient} \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/General/Meaning/Totient.lean\#L8}
