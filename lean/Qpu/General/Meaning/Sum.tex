\paragraph{\texttt{sumZ\_eq\_list\_sum}}
Computes the sum of the elements of a list.  Examples: * `[a, b, c].sum = a + (b + (c + 0))` * `[1, 2, 5].sum = 8`
\[ \forall (l : \mathrm{List} \mathbb{Z}), \mathrm{Qpu.Federated.sumZ}\ l = \mathrm{l.sum} \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/General/Meaning/Sum.lean\#L9}
