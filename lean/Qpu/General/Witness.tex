\paragraph{\texttt{Witness.split\_unique}}
Only one split of a semiprime: for primes `p ≤ q`, if `d * e = p * q` with `1 < d ≤ e`, then `d = p` and `e = q`. Composed from Mathlib's `Nat.dvd\_prime`, `Nat.Prime.pos` and `Nat.Prime.two\_le`, with Lean core's `Nat.dvd\_mul` and `Nat.eq\_of\_mul\_eq\_mul\_left`.
\[ \forall \{p\ q\ d\ e : \mathbb{N}\}, \mathrm{Nat.Prime}\ p \to \mathrm{Nat.Prime}\ q \to p \le q \to d \le e \to d \cdot e = p \cdot q \to 1 < d \to d = p \land e = q \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/General/Witness.lean\#L15}

\paragraph{\texttt{Witness.witness\_unique}}
Only the true witness decodes: for primes `p ≤ q`, a candidate centre `a` with gap `b` that splits `(a - b) * (a + b) = p * q` with `1 < a - b` has `a - b = p` and `a + b = q`. From `Witness.split\_unique`, itself composed from Mathlib's `Nat.dvd\_prime`, `Nat.Prime.pos` and `Nat.Prime.two\_le`.
\[ \forall \{p\ q\ a\ b : \mathbb{N}\}, \mathrm{Nat.Prime}\ p \to \mathrm{Nat.Prime}\ q \to p \le q \to (a - b) \cdot (a + b) = p \cdot q \to 1 < a - b \to a - b = p \land a + b = q \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/General/Witness.lean\#L36}
