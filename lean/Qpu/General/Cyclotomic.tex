\paragraph{\texttt{Polynomial.prod\_cyclotomic\_eq\_X\_pow\_add\_one}}
`X \^{} n + 1` is the product of the cyclotomic polynomials `Φ\_d` over the divisors `d` of `2 n` that do not divide `n`, for `0 < n` over any commutative ring. Proposed for Mathlib beside `prod\_cyclotomic\_eq\_X\_pow\_sub\_one`. Prior art: 8f1972b9-da78-721e-9d7e-cf6e117067ed (lean/dossiers/Polynomial.prod\_cyclotomic\_eq\_X\_pow\_add\_one.md)
\[ \forall \{n : \mathbb{N}\}, 0 < n \to \forall (R : \mathrm{Type}\ \mathrm{u\_1}) [\mathrm{inst} : \mathrm{CommRing}\ R], \prod d \in (2 \cdot n)\mathrm{.divisors} \setminus \mathrm{n.divisors}, \mathrm{Polynomial.cyclotomic}\ d\ R = \mathrm{Polynomial.X} ^{n} + 1 \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/General/Cyclotomic.lean\#L15}
