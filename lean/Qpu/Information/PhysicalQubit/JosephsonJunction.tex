\paragraph{\texttt{JosephsonJunction.flux\_quantum\_definition}}
Φ₀ · 2e = h, exactly: the flux quantum is the Planck constant over twice the elementary charge
\[ \mathrm{JosephsonJunction.phi0Num} \cdot (2 \cdot \mathrm{PhysicalConstant.eNum}) \cdot 10 ^{\mathrm{JosephsonJunction.hExp}} = \mathrm{PhysicalConstant.hNum} \cdot \mathrm{JosephsonJunction.phi0Den} \cdot 10 ^{\mathrm{JosephsonJunction.eExp}} \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Information/PhysicalQubit/JosephsonJunction.lean\#L21}

\paragraph{\texttt{JosephsonJunction.flux\_quantum\_josephson\_reciprocal}}
the Josephson relations are reciprocal: Φ₀ · K\_J = 1
\[ \mathrm{JosephsonJunction.phi0Num} \cdot (2 \cdot \mathrm{PhysicalConstant.eNum} \cdot 10 ^{\mathrm{JosephsonJunction.hExp}}) = \mathrm{JosephsonJunction.phi0Den} \cdot (\mathrm{PhysicalConstant.hNum} \cdot 10 ^{\mathrm{JosephsonJunction.eExp}}) \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Information/PhysicalQubit/JosephsonJunction.lean\#L35}

\paragraph{\texttt{JosephsonJunction.flux\_quantum\_value}}
Φ₀ = 2.067 833 848 … × 10⁻¹⁵ Wb: it lies in [2 067 833 848, 2 067 833 849] × 10⁻²⁴
\[ 2067833848 \cdot \mathrm{JosephsonJunction.phi0Den} \le \mathrm{JosephsonJunction.phi0Num} \cdot 10 ^{24} \land \mathrm{JosephsonJunction.phi0Num} \cdot 10 ^{24} < 2067833849 \cdot \mathrm{JosephsonJunction.phi0Den} \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Information/PhysicalQubit/JosephsonJunction.lean\#L25}

\paragraph{\texttt{JosephsonJunction.josephson\_constant\_value}}
the Josephson constant K\_J = 2e / h = 483 597.848 4 … GHz/V: 2e·10\^{}hExp / (h·10\^{}eExp) in [483 597 848 4, 483 597 848 5] × 10⁻⁴ GHz/V
\[ 4835978484 \cdot (\mathrm{PhysicalConstant.hNum} \cdot 10 ^{\mathrm{JosephsonJunction.eExp}}) \cdot 10 ^{9} \le 2 \cdot \mathrm{PhysicalConstant.eNum} \cdot 10 ^{\mathrm{JosephsonJunction.hExp}} \cdot 10 ^{4} \land 2 \cdot \mathrm{PhysicalConstant.eNum} \cdot 10 ^{\mathrm{JosephsonJunction.hExp}} \cdot 10 ^{4} < 4835978485 \cdot (\mathrm{PhysicalConstant.hNum} \cdot 10 ^{\mathrm{JosephsonJunction.eExp}}) \cdot 10 ^{9} \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Information/PhysicalQubit/JosephsonJunction.lean\#L30}
