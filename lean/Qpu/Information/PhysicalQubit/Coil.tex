\paragraph{\texttt{Coil.periodic}}
the qubit reads the flux modulo one flux quantum: a whole flux quantum added leaves it unchanged, for every M and every current
\[ \forall (M\ I\ k : \mathbb{N}), (\mathrm{Coil.flux}\ M\ I + k \cdot (\mathrm{PhysicalConstant.hNum} \cdot 10 ^{10})) \bmod (\mathrm{PhysicalConstant.hNum} \cdot 10 ^{10}) = \mathrm{Coil.flux}\ M\ I \bmod (\mathrm{PhysicalConstant.hNum} \cdot 10 ^{10}) \]
