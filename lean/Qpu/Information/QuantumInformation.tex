\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumInformation.no\_cloning}}
no-cloning: every linear map sending |0⟩|0⟩ to |0⟩|0⟩ and |1⟩|0⟩ to |1⟩|1⟩ fails to copy |+⟩: |+⟩|0⟩ does not go to |+⟩|+⟩
\[ \forall (u : \mathrm{Operator.Mat}), (\forall r < 4, \mathrm{Operator.ap}\ 4\ u (\mathrm{HilbertSpace.basis}\ 0) r = \mathrm{HilbertSpace.basis}\ 0\ r) \to (\forall r < 4, \mathrm{Operator.ap}\ 4\ u (\mathrm{HilbertSpace.basis}\ 2) r = \mathrm{HilbertSpace.basis}\ 3\ r) \to \mathrm{Operator.ap}\ 4\ u (\lambda \ r \mapsto \mathrm{HilbertSpace.basis}\ 0\ r + \mathrm{HilbertSpace.basis}\ 2\ r) 1 \ne 1 \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Information/QuantumInformation.lean\#L14}
