\paragraph{\texttt{shor}}
\[ \mathrm{period}\ \mathrm{shorBase}\ \mathrm{shorModulus} \bmod \mathrm{coins} = 0 \land \mathrm{half}\ \mathrm{shorBase}\ \mathrm{shorModulus} < \mathrm{shorModulus} - \mathrm{seed} \land \mathrm{seed} < \mathrm{\_root\_.gcd} (\mathrm{half}\ \mathrm{shorBase}\ \mathrm{shorModulus} - \mathrm{seed}) \mathrm{shorModulus} \land \mathrm{\_root\_.gcd} (\mathrm{half}\ \mathrm{shorBase}\ \mathrm{shorModulus} - \mathrm{seed}) \mathrm{shorModulus} < \mathrm{shorModulus} \land \mathrm{\_root\_.gcd} (\mathrm{half}\ \mathrm{shorBase}\ \mathrm{shorModulus} - \mathrm{seed}) \mathrm{shorModulus} \cdot \mathrm{\_root\_.gcd} (\mathrm{half}\ \mathrm{shorBase}\ \mathrm{shorModulus} + \mathrm{seed}) \mathrm{shorModulus} = \mathrm{shorModulus} \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Application/Shor.lean\#L60}

\paragraph{\texttt{shor\_cases}}
Every door, including moduli past the five-bit comparison 21. 2 mod 35 has order 12 and splits into 7 × 5. 2 mod 77 has order 30 and splits into 7 × 11. 2 mod 7 has odd order 3, so the half-root step does not run. 3 mod 7 has order 6 and 3³ ≡ −1, so the root is trivial. gcd(14, 21) = 7 is Euclid, before any period. 2048 = 2¹¹ is trial, not a period.
\[ \mathrm{period}\ 2\ 35 = 12 \land \mathrm{\_root\_.gcd} (\mathrm{powMod}\ 2\ 6\ 35 - 1) 35 = 7 \land \mathrm{\_root\_.gcd} (\mathrm{powMod}\ 2\ 6\ 35 + 1) 35 = 5 \land \mathrm{period}\ 2\ 77 = 30 \land \mathrm{\_root\_.gcd} (\mathrm{powMod}\ 2\ 15\ 77 - 1) 77 = 7 \land \mathrm{\_root\_.gcd} (\mathrm{powMod}\ 2\ 15\ 77 + 1) 77 = 11 \land \mathrm{period}\ 2\ 7 = 3 \land \mathrm{period}\ 2\ 7 \bmod \mathrm{coins} = 1 \land \mathrm{period}\ 3\ 7 = 6 \land \mathrm{powMod}\ 3\ 3\ 7 + 1 = 7 \land \mathrm{\_root\_.gcd}\ 14\ 21 = 7 \land 2048 = 2 ^{11} \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Application/Shor.lean\#L71}
