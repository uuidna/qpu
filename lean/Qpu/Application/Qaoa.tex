\paragraph{\texttt{qaoaParams\_succ}}
Every layer adds exactly coins = 2 parameters, for every p.
\[ \forall (p : \mathbb{N}), \mathrm{qaoaParams} (p + 1) = \mathrm{qaoaParams}\ p + \mathrm{coins} \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Application/Qaoa.lean\#L21}

\paragraph{\texttt{qaoaX\_succ\_layer}}
One more layer costs q more mixer gates, for every qubit count.
\[ \forall (p\ q : \mathbb{N}), \mathrm{qaoaX} (p + 1) q = \mathrm{qaoaX}\ p\ q + q \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Application/Qaoa.lean\#L33}

\paragraph{\texttt{qaoaZz\_le\_edges}}
Denser graphs never cost fewer ZZ gates, for all edge pairs at any depth.
\[ \forall (p : \mathbb{N}) \{e\ e' : \mathbb{N}\}, e \le e' \to \mathrm{qaoaZz}\ p\ e \le \mathrm{qaoaZz}\ p\ e' \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Application/Qaoa.lean\#L42}

\paragraph{\texttt{qaoaZz\_le\_layers}}
Deeper circuits never cost fewer ZZ gates, for all layer pairs and edge sets.
\[ \forall \{p\ p' : \mathbb{N}\}, p \le p' \to \forall (e : \mathbb{N}), \mathrm{qaoaZz}\ p\ e \le \mathrm{qaoaZz}\ p'\ e \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Application/Qaoa.lean\#L37}

\paragraph{\texttt{qaoaZz\_succ\_edge}}
One more edge costs p more ZZ gates, for every edge count and layer depth.
\[ \forall (p\ e : \mathbb{N}), \mathrm{qaoaZz}\ p (e + 1) = \mathrm{qaoaZz}\ p\ e + p \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Application/Qaoa.lean\#L29}

\paragraph{\texttt{qaoaZz\_succ\_layer}}
One more layer costs e more ZZ gates, for every layer count and edge set.
\[ \forall (p\ e : \mathbb{N}), \mathrm{qaoaZz} (p + 1) e = \mathrm{qaoaZz}\ p\ e + e \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Application/Qaoa.lean\#L25}

\paragraph{\texttt{qaoa\_all}}
The textbook spot values, decided by the kernel as controls on the general laws.
\[ \mathrm{qaoaParams}\ 3 = 6 \land \mathrm{qaoaZz}\ 2\ 5 = 10 \land \mathrm{qaoaX}\ 3\ 4 = 12 \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Application/Qaoa.lean\#L52}

\paragraph{\texttt{qaoa\_budget\_per\_layer}}
The budget splits per layer: ZZ cost plus X mixers over p layers is p copies of the single-layer count, for every p, e and q.
\[ \forall (p\ e\ q : \mathbb{N}), \mathrm{qaoaZz}\ p\ e + \mathrm{qaoaX}\ p\ q = p \cdot (e + q) \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Application/Qaoa.lean\#L48}
