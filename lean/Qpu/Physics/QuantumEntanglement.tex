\paragraph{\texttt{Qpu.Verify.Entangle.ghz\_quantum\_wins\_all\_four}}
The entangled GHZ trinity strategy wins 4/4 — strictly above the classical ceiling of 3.
\[ \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Entangle.quantumCheck}\ 0\ \mathrm{false}\ \mathrm{false}\ \mathrm{false} + \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Entangle.quantumCheck}\ 1\ \mathrm{false}\ \mathrm{true}\ \mathrm{true} + \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Entangle.quantumCheck}\ 2\ \mathrm{true}\ \mathrm{false}\ \mathrm{true} + \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Entangle.quantumCheck}\ 3\ \mathrm{true}\ \mathrm{true}\ \mathrm{false} = 4 \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Physics/QuantumEntanglement.lean\#L93}

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumEntanglement.chsh\_classical\_bound}}
every assignment of definite values gives |S| = 2: the classical bound
\[ \forall (\mathrm{a0}\ \mathrm{a1}\ \mathrm{b0}\ \mathrm{b1} : \mathrm{Bool}), \mathrm{QuantumEntanglement.chshClassical}\ \mathrm{a0}\ \mathrm{a1}\ \mathrm{b0}\ \mathrm{b1} = 2 \lor \mathrm{QuantumEntanglement.chshClassical}\ \mathrm{a0}\ \mathrm{a1}\ \mathrm{b0}\ \mathrm{b1} = -2 \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Physics/QuantumEntanglement.lean\#L30}

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumEntanglement.chsh\_quantum\_value}}
the Bell state gives S² = 8: the scaled sum is 8 = S · ⟨Φ|Φ⟩ · √2, so S² = 8² / (2² · 2) = 8, beyond the classical 2² = 4
\[ \mathrm{QuantumEntanglement.chshBell} = 8 \land 8 ^{2} = 8 \cdot (2 ^{2} \cdot 2) \land 2 ^{2} < 8 \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Physics/QuantumEntanglement.lean\#L41}
