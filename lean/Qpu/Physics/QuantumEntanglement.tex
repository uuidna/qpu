\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumEntanglement.bell\_state}}
the Bell state is supported on exactly 00 and 11, with equal weight
\[ (\forall r < 4, \mathrm{QuantumEntanglement.bell}\ r \ne 0 \leftrightarrow r = 0 \lor r = 3) \land (\mathrm{QuantumEntanglement.bell}\ 0)\mathrm{.normSq} = (\mathrm{QuantumEntanglement.bell}\ 3)\mathrm{.normSq} \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Physics/QuantumEntanglement.lean\#L20}

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumEntanglement.chsh\_classical\_bound}}
every assignment of definite values gives |S| = 2: the classical bound
\[ \forall (\mathrm{a0}\ \mathrm{a1}\ \mathrm{b0}\ \mathrm{b1} : \mathrm{Bool}), \mathrm{QuantumEntanglement.chshClassical}\ \mathrm{a0}\ \mathrm{a1}\ \mathrm{b0}\ \mathrm{b1} = 2 \lor \mathrm{QuantumEntanglement.chshClassical}\ \mathrm{a0}\ \mathrm{a1}\ \mathrm{b0}\ \mathrm{b1} = -2 \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Physics/QuantumEntanglement.lean\#L34}

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumEntanglement.chsh\_quantum\_value}}
the Bell state gives S² = 8: the scaled sum is 8 = S · ⟨Φ|Φ⟩ · √2, so S² = 8² / (2² · 2) = 8, beyond the classical 2² = 4
\[ \mathrm{QuantumEntanglement.chshBell} = 8 \land 8 ^{2} = 8 \cdot (2 ^{2} \cdot 2) \land 2 ^{2} < 8 \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Physics/QuantumEntanglement.lean\#L45}

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumEntanglement.ghz\_state}}
the GHZ state is supported on exactly 000 and 111, with equal weight

\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Physics/QuantumEntanglement.lean\#L26}
