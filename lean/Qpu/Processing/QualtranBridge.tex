\paragraph{\texttt{QualtranBridge.z\_powers\_add}}
Powers of `Z` add: `e\^{}\{iπ(a+b)\} = e\^{}\{iπa\} e\^{}\{iπb\}`, so `Z\^{}\{3/4\} = S·T` and `Z\^{}\{5/4\} = Z·T` each cost one T gate beside a Clifford. The exponent distributes over the sum (`mul\_add`, `Complex.ofReal\_add`, `add\_mul`, through `Distrib.leftDistribClass` and `Distrib.rightDistribClass`), and the exponential of a sum is the product (`Complex.exp\_add`).
\[ \forall (a\ b : \mathbb{R}), \mathrm{Complex.exp} (\uparrow (\mathrm{Real.pi} \cdot (a + b)) \cdot \mathrm{Complex.I}) = \mathrm{Complex.exp} (\uparrow (\mathrm{Real.pi} \cdot a) \cdot \mathrm{Complex.I}) \cdot \mathrm{Complex.exp} (\uparrow (\mathrm{Real.pi} \cdot b) \cdot \mathrm{Complex.I}) \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QualtranBridge.lean\#L18}
