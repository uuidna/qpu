\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumCircuit.needs\_routing}}
along a register row, bits 0 and 1 and bits 1 and 2 are coupled, bits 0 and 2 are not: the gate between them needs routing
\[ (0, 0) \in \mathrm{QuantumProcessor.sites}\ \mathrm{hexbit} \land (0, 2) \in \mathrm{QuantumProcessor.sites}\ \mathrm{hexbit} \land \mathrm{QuantumProcessor.coupled} (0, 0) (0, 1) = \mathrm{true} \land \mathrm{QuantumProcessor.coupled} (0, 1) (0, 2) = \mathrm{true} \land \mathrm{QuantumProcessor.coupled} (0, 0) (0, 2) = \mathrm{false} \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumCircuit.lean\#L24}

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumCircuit.routed\_depth}}
a routed CNOT at distance `d ≥ 1` takes `2·d − 1` gates in sequence, so its depth is at most `2·d − 1`; at distance 2 that is the three gates of `swap\_routing`
\[ \forall (d : \mathbb{N}), 1 \le d \to \mathrm{QuantumCircuit.routedGates}\ d = 2 \cdot d - 1 \land \mathrm{QuantumCircuit.routedGates}\ 2 = 3 \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumCircuit.lean\#L36}

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumCircuit.swap\_routing}}
routing preserves the function: SWAP(0,1) · CNOT(1,2) · SWAP(0,1) is exactly CNOT(0,2)
\[ \mathrm{Operator.Same}\ 8 (\mathrm{Operator.mm}\ 8 (\mathrm{Operator.mm}\ 8\ \mathrm{QuantumCircuit.swap01}\ \mathrm{QuantumCircuit.cnot12}) \mathrm{QuantumCircuit.swap01}) \mathrm{QuantumCircuit.cnot02} \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumCircuit.lean\#L29}
