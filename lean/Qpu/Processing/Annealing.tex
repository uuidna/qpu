\paragraph{\texttt{annealing\_all}}
The textbook spot values, decided by the kernel as controls on the general laws.
\[ \mathrm{chimera}\ 16 = 2048 \land \mathrm{pegasus}\ 16 = 5760 \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/Annealing.lean\#L55}

\paragraph{\texttt{chimera\_monotone}}
Chimera counts never shrink with more cells, for all size pairs.
\[ \forall \{m\ m' : \mathbb{N}\}, m \le m' \to \mathrm{chimera}\ m \le \mathrm{chimera}\ m' \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/Annealing.lean\#L36}

\paragraph{\texttt{chimera\_pos}}
A nonempty topology has qubits, for every cell size m ≥ 1.
\[ \forall (m : \mathbb{N}), 0 < m \to 0 < \mathrm{chimera}\ m \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/Annealing.lean\#L32}

\paragraph{\texttt{chimera\_succ}}
One more cell row adds exactly vertices·(2m+1) qubits, for every m.
\[ \forall (m : \mathbb{N}), \mathrm{chimera} (m + 1) = \mathrm{chimera}\ m + \mathrm{vertices} \cdot (2 \cdot m + 1) \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/Annealing.lean\#L25}

\paragraph{\texttt{pegasus\_monotone}}
Pegasus counts never shrink with more cells: the m·(m−1) factor is monotone cell by cell, for all size pairs.
\[ \forall \{m\ m' : \mathbb{N}\}, m \le m' \to \mathrm{pegasus}\ m \le \mathrm{pegasus}\ m' \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/Annealing.lean\#L42}

\paragraph{\texttt{pegasus\_pos}}
Two or more cells always hold Pegasus qubits, uniformly in m.
\[ \forall (m : \mathbb{N}), 1 < m \to 0 < \mathrm{pegasus}\ m \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/Annealing.lean\#L48}
