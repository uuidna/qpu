\paragraph{\texttt{cliffordOrder\_pos}}
The n-qubit Clifford group is nonempty, for every n.
\[ \forall (n : \mathbb{N}), 0 < \mathrm{cliffordOrder}\ n \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/Clifford.lean\#L52}

\paragraph{\texttt{clifford\_all}}
The textbook spot values, decided by the kernel as controls on the general laws.
\[ \mathrm{symplectic}\ 1 = 6 \land \mathrm{symplectic}\ 2 = 720 \land \mathrm{symplectic}\ 3 = 1451520 \land \mathrm{cliffordOrder}\ 2 = 11520 \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/Clifford.lean\#L61}

\paragraph{\texttt{sympFactor\_ge\_two}}
Each symplectic factor 4\^{}j − 1 is at least 2 − 1, for every j.
\[ \forall (j : \mathbb{N}), 2 \le \mathrm{mint} (2 \cdot (j + 1)) \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/Clifford.lean\#L34}

\paragraph{\texttt{sympProd\_grows}}
The symplectic product never shrinks across a factor, for every j.
\[ \forall (j : \mathbb{N}), \mathrm{sympProd}\ j \le \mathrm{sympProd} (j + 1) \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/Clifford.lean\#L56}

\paragraph{\texttt{sympProd\_pos}}
The symplectic product never vanishes, for every j.
\[ \forall (j : \mathbb{N}), 0 < \mathrm{sympProd}\ j \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/Clifford.lean\#L40}

\paragraph{\texttt{sympProd\_step}}
One symplectic factor, for every j.
\[ \forall (j : \mathbb{N}), \mathrm{sympProd} (j + 1) = (\mathrm{mint} (2 \cdot (j + 1)) - 1) \cdot \mathrm{sympProd}\ j \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/Clifford.lean\#L31}

\paragraph{\texttt{symplectic\_pos}}
Sp(2n, 2) is a nonempty group, for every n.
\[ \forall (n : \mathbb{N}), 0 < \mathrm{symplectic}\ n \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/Clifford.lean\#L48}
