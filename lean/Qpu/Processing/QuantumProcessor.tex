\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumProcessor.connected}}
every site of the register's grid is reached from (0, 0): the coupling graph is connected
\[ \forall x \in \mathrm{QuantumProcessor.sites}\ \mathrm{hexbit}, x \in \mathrm{QuantumProcessor.reach}\ \mathrm{hexbit} ((\mathrm{Qubit.rows}\ \mathrm{hexbit})\mathrm{.length} + 4) \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumProcessor.lean\#L41}

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumProcessor.degree\_bounds}}
every site has at least two and at most four couplers
\[ \forall x \in \mathrm{QuantumProcessor.sites}\ \mathrm{hexbit}, 2 \le \mathrm{QuantumProcessor.degree}\ \mathrm{hexbit}\ x \land \mathrm{QuantumProcessor.degree}\ \mathrm{hexbit}\ x \le 4 \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumProcessor.lean\#L47}

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumProcessor.sites\_count}}
the register's grid has `effective hexbit` sites
\[ (\mathrm{QuantumProcessor.sites}\ \mathrm{hexbit})\mathrm{.length} = \mathrm{Qubit.effective}\ \mathrm{hexbit} \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumProcessor.lean\#L38}
