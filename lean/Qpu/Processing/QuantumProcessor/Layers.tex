\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumProcessor.Layers.layers\_bipartite}}
every coupler joins the two colours: the coupling graph is bipartite
\[ \forall e \in \mathrm{QuantumProcessor.Layers.edges}\ \mathrm{hexbit}, \mathrm{QuantumProcessor.Layers.colour}\ \mathrm{e.1} \ne \mathrm{QuantumProcessor.Layers.colour}\ \mathrm{e.2} \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumProcessor/Layers.lean\#L34}

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumProcessor.Layers.layers\_count}}
the couplers so counted are the register's couplers at hexbit width
\[ (\mathrm{QuantumProcessor.Layers.edges}\ \mathrm{hexbit})\mathrm{.length} = \mathrm{Qubit.couplers}\ \mathrm{hexbit} \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumProcessor/Layers.lean\#L47}

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumProcessor.Layers.layers\_four}}
every coupler sits in one of four layers, and within a layer no qubit is touched twice: four rounds drive every coupler
\[ (\forall e \in \mathrm{QuantumProcessor.Layers.edges}\ \mathrm{hexbit}, \mathrm{QuantumProcessor.Layers.layer}\ e < 4) \land \forall x \in \mathrm{QuantumProcessor.sites}\ \mathrm{hexbit}, \forall l < 4, \mathrm{QuantumProcessor.Layers.touching}\ \mathrm{hexbit}\ l\ x \le 1 \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumProcessor/Layers.lean\#L39}
