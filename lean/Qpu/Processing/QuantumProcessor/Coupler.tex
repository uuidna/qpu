\paragraph{\texttt{Coupler.off}}
the coupling is off at detuning Δ when the direct and mediated terms cancel, for every design
\[ \forall (g_{1} g_{2} g_{12} \Delta : \mathbb{N}), g_{12} \cdot \Delta = g_{1} \cdot g_{2} \to g_{1} \cdot g_{2} - g_{12} \cdot \Delta = 0 \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumProcessor/Coupler.lean\#L13}

Willow's measured CZ times, 42 ns and 37 ns, put the exchange coupling at 8.4 MHz and 9.5 MHz
\[ \mathrm{Coupler.cz}\ 42\ 84 \land \mathrm{Coupler.cz}\ 37\ 95 \]
