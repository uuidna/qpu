\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumGate.cnot}}
CNOT is unitary and its own inverse: CNOT · CNOT† = I and CNOT · CNOT = I
\[ \mathrm{Operator.Same}\ 4 (\mathrm{Operator.mm}\ 4\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.CNOT} (\mathrm{Operator.dag}\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.CNOT})) \mathrm{Operator.idm} \land \mathrm{Operator.Same}\ 4 (\mathrm{Operator.mm}\ 4\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.CNOT}\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.CNOT}) \mathrm{Operator.idm} \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumGate.lean\#L41}

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumGate.h\_squared}}
Hadamard squared is twice the identity: H · H = 2 · I
\[ \mathrm{Operator.Same}\ 2 (\mathrm{Operator.mm}\ 2\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.H}\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.H}) (\mathrm{Operator.smul}\ 2\ \mathrm{Operator.idm}) \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumGate.lean\#L35}

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumGate.h\_unitary}}
Hadamard is unitary up to its scale: H · H† = 2 · I
\[ \mathrm{Operator.Same}\ 2 (\mathrm{Operator.mm}\ 2\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.H} (\mathrm{Operator.dag}\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.H})) (\mathrm{Operator.smul}\ 2\ \mathrm{Operator.idm}) \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumGate.lean\#L33}

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumGate.paulis\_unitary}}
X, Z and S are unitary: U · U† = I
\[ \mathrm{Operator.Same}\ 2 (\mathrm{Operator.mm}\ 2\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.X} (\mathrm{Operator.dag}\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.X})) \mathrm{Operator.idm} \land \mathrm{Operator.Same}\ 2 (\mathrm{Operator.mm}\ 2\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.Z} (\mathrm{Operator.dag}\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.Z})) \mathrm{Operator.idm} \land \mathrm{Operator.Same}\ 2 (\mathrm{Operator.mm}\ 2\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.S} (\mathrm{Operator.dag}\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.S})) \mathrm{Operator.idm} \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumGate.lean\#L37}

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumGate.s\_squared}}

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumGate.t\_gate}}
T is unitary, has order 8 (T⁸ = I and T⁴ ≠ I) and does not commute with Hadamard
\[ \mathrm{QuantumGate.SameW} (\mathrm{QuantumGate.mmW}\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.TW} (\mathrm{QuantumGate.dagW}\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.TW})) \mathrm{QuantumGate.idW} \land \mathrm{QuantumGate.SameW} (\mathrm{QuantumGate.powW}\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.TW}\ 8) \mathrm{QuantumGate.idW} \land \neg \mathrm{QuantumGate.SameW} (\mathrm{QuantumGate.powW}\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.TW}\ 4) \mathrm{QuantumGate.idW} \land \neg \mathrm{QuantumGate.SameW} (\mathrm{QuantumGate.mmW}\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.HW}\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.TW}) (\mathrm{QuantumGate.mmW}\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.TW}\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.HW}) \]
