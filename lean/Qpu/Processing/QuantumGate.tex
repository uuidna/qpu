\paragraph{\texttt{Qpu.Verify.Gate.cnot\_is\_an\_involution}}
CNOT² = I₄: entangling twice un-entangles.
\[ \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.cnot} \cdot \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.cnot} = \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.id4} \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumGate.lean\#L224}

\paragraph{\texttt{Qpu.Verify.Gate.cnot\_is\_not\_swap}}
Negative control: CNOT and SWAP are genuinely different gates.
\[ \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.cnot} \ne \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.swap} \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumGate.lean\#L228}

\paragraph{\texttt{Qpu.Verify.Gate.h\_unitary}}
(√2·H)² = 2·I — Hadamard unitarity in scaled units.
\[ \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.scaledH} \cdot \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.scaledH} = \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.M2.two} \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumGate.lean\#L33}

\paragraph{\texttt{Qpu.Verify.Gate.h\_x\_h\_is\_not\_two\_y}}
Negative control: H X H = 2Z, never 2Y.
\[ \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.scaledH} \cdot \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.matX} \cdot \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.scaledH} \ne \{ \mathrm{a00} := \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.g}\ 0, \mathrm{a01} := \{ \mathrm{re} := 0, \mathrm{im} := -2 \}, \mathrm{a10} := \{ \mathrm{re} := 0, \mathrm{im} := 2 \}, \mathrm{a11} := \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.g}\ 0 \} \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumGate.lean\#L169}

\paragraph{\texttt{Qpu.Verify.Gate.h\_z\_h\_is\_two\_x}}
H Z H = X in scaled units: (√2 H)·Z·(√2 H) = 2X.
\[ \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.scaledH} \cdot \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.matZ} \cdot \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.scaledH} = \{ \mathrm{a00} := \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.g}\ 0, \mathrm{a01} := \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.g}\ 2, \mathrm{a10} := \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.g}\ 2, \mathrm{a11} := \mathrm{Qpu.Verify.Gate.g}\ 0 \} \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumGate.lean\#L167}

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumGate.cnot}}
CNOT is unitary and its own inverse: CNOT · CNOT† = I and CNOT · CNOT = I
\[ \mathrm{Operator.Same}\ 4 (\mathrm{Operator.mm}\ 4\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.CNOT} (\mathrm{Operator.dag}\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.CNOT})) \mathrm{Operator.idm} \land \mathrm{Operator.Same}\ 4 (\mathrm{Operator.mm}\ 4\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.CNOT}\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.CNOT}) \mathrm{Operator.idm} \]
\noindent axioms: none; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/QuantumGate.lean\#L41}

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumGate.h\_squared}}
Hadamard squared is twice the identity: H · H = 2 · I
\[ \mathrm{Operator.Same}\ 2 (\mathrm{Operator.mm}\ 2\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.H}\ \mathrm{QuantumGate.H}) (\mathrm{Operator.smul}\ 2\ \mathrm{Operator.idm}) \]

\paragraph{\texttt{QuantumGate.h\_unitary}}
