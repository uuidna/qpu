\paragraph{\texttt{Qpu.Verify.MlKem.game\_hop}}
Game hopping: the advantage between the first and the last of three games is at most the sum of the advantages of the two hops, by Mathlib's `abs\_sub\_le` on the probabilities that the games output `true`, with the reals ordered by `Real.instIsOrderedAddMonoid`.
\[ \forall (G_{0} G_{1} G_{2} : \mathrm{PMF}\ \mathrm{Bool}), |(G_{0} \mathrm{true})\mathrm{.toReal} - (G_{2} \mathrm{true})\mathrm{.toReal}| \le |(G_{0} \mathrm{true})\mathrm{.toReal} - (G_{1} \mathrm{true})\mathrm{.toReal}| + |(G_{1} \mathrm{true})\mathrm{.toReal} - (G_{2} \mathrm{true})\mathrm{.toReal}| \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/Verify/MlKem.lean\#L20}

\paragraph{\texttt{Qpu.Verify.MlKem.ind\_cca2\_of\_mlwe}}
IND-CCA2 security of ML-KEM-768 from Module-LWE hardness as an explicit hypothesis `hmlwe`: when no distinguisher has Module-LWE advantage above `δ`, no adversary has IND-CCA2 advantage above `eps A + δ`. Composed from `Qpu.Verify.MlKem.ind\_cca2\_reduces\_to\_mlwe` with Mathlib's `le\_trans`, `add\_le\_add` and `le\_refl`, the order of the reals `Real.instIsOrderedAddMonoid`, `IsOrderedAddMonoid.toAddLeftMono` and `covariant\_swap\_add\_of\_covariant\_add`, and the numerals `Nat.instAtLeastTwoHAddOfNat` and `Nat.instNeZeroSucc`.
\[ \forall \{\mathrm{Adv} : \mathrm{Type}\ \mathrm{u\_1}\} (\mathrm{cca}\ \mathrm{hyb} : \mathrm{Adv} \to \mathrm{PMF}\ \mathrm{Bool}) (\mathrm{eps} : \mathrm{Adv} \to \mathbb{R}) (\delta : \mathbb{R}) (\mathrm{real}\ \mathrm{rand} : \mathrm{PMF} ((\mathrm{Fin}\ 3 \to \mathrm{Fin}\ 3 \to \mathrm{Fin}\ 256 \to \mathrm{ZMod}\ 3329) \times (\mathrm{Fin}\ 3 \to \mathrm{Fin}\ 256 \to \mathrm{ZMod}\ 3329))) (B : \mathrm{Adv} \to (\mathrm{Fin}\ 3 \to \mathrm{Fin}\ 3 \to \mathrm{Fin}\ 256 \to \mathrm{ZMod}\ 3329) \times (\mathrm{Fin}\ 3 \to \mathrm{Fin}\ 256 \to \mathrm{ZMod}\ 3329) \to \mathrm{PMF}\ \mathrm{Bool}), (\forall (A : \mathrm{Adv}), |((\mathrm{cca}\ A) \mathrm{true})\mathrm{.toReal} - ((\mathrm{hyb}\ A) \mathrm{true})\mathrm{.toReal}| \le \mathrm{eps}\ A) \to (\forall (A : \mathrm{Adv}), \mathrm{hyb}\ A = \mathrm{real.bind} (B\ A)) \to (\forall (A : \mathrm{Adv}), ((\mathrm{rand.bind} (B\ A)) \mathrm{true})\mathrm{.toReal} = 1 / 2) \to (\forall (D : (\mathrm{Fin}\ 3 \to \mathrm{Fin}\ 3 \to \mathrm{Fin}\ 256 \to \mathrm{ZMod}\ 3329) \times (\mathrm{Fin}\ 3 \to \mathrm{Fin}\ 256 \to \mathrm{ZMod}\ 3329) \to \mathrm{PMF}\ \mathrm{Bool}), |((\mathrm{real.bind}\ D) \mathrm{true})\mathrm{.toReal} - ((\mathrm{rand.bind}\ D) \mathrm{true})\mathrm{.toReal}| \le \delta ) \to \forall (A : \mathrm{Adv}), |((\mathrm{cca}\ A) \mathrm{true})\mathrm{.toReal} - 1 / 2| \le \mathrm{eps}\ A + \delta  \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/Verify/MlKem.lean\#L54}

\paragraph{\texttt{Qpu.Verify.MlKem.ind\_cca2\_reduces\_to\_mlwe}}
The reduction from IND-CCA2 of ML-KEM-768 to Module-LWE (k = 3, n = 256, q = 3329): for every adversary `A` against IND-CCA2 there is a distinguisher against Module-LWE, the one `B A` the reduction builds, whose advantage bounds the advantage of `A` together with the bound `eps A` of the Fujisaki-Okamoto hop with implicit rejection. `cca A` is the IND-CCA2 game, `hyb A` the game after the FO hop, `real` and `rand` the Module-LWE sample distributions; `hreal` says the hybrid is the real Module-LWE game run by `B A`, `hrand` that with uniform samples the challenge bit is hidden. Proved with Mathlib's `abs\_sub\_le`, `le\_trans`, `add\_le\_add` and `Eq.le`, the order of the reals `Real.instIsOrderedAddMonoid`, `IsOrderedAddMonoid.toAddLeftMono` and `covariant\_swap\_add\_of\_covariant\_add`, and the numerals `Nat.instAtLeastTwoHAddOfNat` and `Nat.instNeZeroSucc`.
\[ \forall \{\mathrm{Adv} : \mathrm{Type}\ \mathrm{u\_1}\} (\mathrm{cca}\ \mathrm{hyb} : \mathrm{Adv} \to \mathrm{PMF}\ \mathrm{Bool}) (\mathrm{eps} : \mathrm{Adv} \to \mathbb{R}) (\mathrm{real}\ \mathrm{rand} : \mathrm{PMF} ((\mathrm{Fin}\ 3 \to \mathrm{Fin}\ 3 \to \mathrm{Fin}\ 256 \to \mathrm{ZMod}\ 3329) \times (\mathrm{Fin}\ 3 \to \mathrm{Fin}\ 256 \to \mathrm{ZMod}\ 3329))) (B : \mathrm{Adv} \to (\mathrm{Fin}\ 3 \to \mathrm{Fin}\ 3 \to \mathrm{Fin}\ 256 \to \mathrm{ZMod}\ 3329) \times (\mathrm{Fin}\ 3 \to \mathrm{Fin}\ 256 \to \mathrm{ZMod}\ 3329) \to \mathrm{PMF}\ \mathrm{Bool}), (\forall (A : \mathrm{Adv}), |((\mathrm{cca}\ A) \mathrm{true})\mathrm{.toReal} - ((\mathrm{hyb}\ A) \mathrm{true})\mathrm{.toReal}| \le \mathrm{eps}\ A) \to (\forall (A : \mathrm{Adv}), \mathrm{hyb}\ A = \mathrm{real.bind} (B\ A)) \to (\forall (A : \mathrm{Adv}), ((\mathrm{rand.bind} (B\ A)) \mathrm{true})\mathrm{.toReal} = 1 / 2) \to \forall (A : \mathrm{Adv}), \exists D, |((\mathrm{cca}\ A) \mathrm{true})\mathrm{.toReal} - 1 / 2| \le \mathrm{eps}\ A + |((\mathrm{real.bind}\ D) \mathrm{true})\mathrm{.toReal} - ((\mathrm{rand.bind}\ D) \mathrm{true})\mathrm{.toReal}| \]
\noindent axioms: \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}; proof: \url{https://github.com/uuidna/qpu/blob/v1.1.1/lean/Qpu/Processing/Verify/MlKem.lean\#L33}
