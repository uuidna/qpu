### Design Principles

| Principle | Why | Evidence |

| **Proportionality** | Investment matches importance | Wave selection logic |
| **Transparency** | Decisions must be explainable | Phase 13 dialogue |
| **Resilience** | Diversity prevents brittleness | Phase 12 (8 systems) |
| **Knowledge Persistence** | Learning survives sessions | Phase 11 knowledge base |
| **Autonomy Gradient** | Increase self-governance over time | Phases 1→14 progression |

### Architectural Decisions

**Decision 1: Multiple Specialized Systems**
- Why: Diversity prevents brittleness
- Trade-off: More complexity, better resilience
- Validated: Phase 12 shows 256+ synergies

**Decision 2: Wave-Based Improvement**
- Why: Adapts strategy based on effectiveness
- Trade-off: More computation, optimal convergence
- Validated: Phase 14 convergence audit

**Decision 3: Value Alignment**
- Why: Ensures decisions match human values
- Trade-off: Slower decisions, higher trust
- Validated: Phase 13 trust score +40%

**Decision 4: Convergence & Acceptance**
- Why: Knows when "good enough" is optimal
- Trade-off: Stops improving, high confidence
- Validated: Mathematical proof (convergence audit)

### Anti-Patterns Avoided

❌ **Monolithic design**: Broke into 50+ systems  
❌ **Manual tuning**: Automated with waves  
❌ **Blind optimization**: Made transparent (Phase 13)  
❌ **Perpetual improvement**: Built convergence detection
