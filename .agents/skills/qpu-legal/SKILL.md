## Examples

- `patents-plan`: Every patent API found: the two that answer in the open (Google Patents' query endpoint, the EPO publication server) as requests, the five that take a key or a session named with their variable names, the registry's patent MCPs, and the three theorems every patent read is verified against.
- `patents-read`: Two patents read from a fixture of Google Patents' answer and verified: the Quantinuum patent passes every theorem; the second names a priority after its filing and a grant beyond the twenty-year term — violations named, never hidden.
- `compete-news-plan`: The public news APIs wired as adapters of the DOI research: four open (GDELT, Hacker News, Crossref Event Data, Wikipedia) answering mentions of each record of precedence without a key, three keyed (NewsAPI, The Guardian, The New York Times) waiting for a tenant's name; twelve requests for three records, none capped.
- `compete-mentions`: Fixture answers of the news adapters read into mentions by record and by source — five mentions, one source unreachable — and the acceptance rule of the Clay competition reading them: measured, never a verdict.
- `compete-rules`: Every competition with a prize or funding judged by its own rules: the Clay conditions (three, all required), XPRIZE Quantum Applications closed until spring 2027, Vercel's program closed until its next cohort, the other funding programs pending the author's acts; nothing claimed, the gaps named.
- `legal-audit-offline`: Phase two with no answers (offline): the claims, the tree's security checks, and the audit's own content address over the SHA-256 of every input — change a source and the address changes.
- `legal-court`: Cheap gates leading every violator to court via its own MCP: a refusal at any gate (here a deletion refused because two entries link the one) is answered with the case computed from the refusal alone — the rule violated (links), the theorem that states it, the remedy, the case's content address — and the MCP calls the violator makes: to file the case in the docket (kind case, with its own token or the Payload MCP) or to appeal through the match tool; the gate writes nothing, however many violators.
- `legal-court-rules`: The court's rules: ten ways a gate refuses, each named by its theorem and its remedy, from the shape of a request to the integrity of a fold.
- `legal-court-denier`: Every denial identifies who denies and shows the legal grounds of all involved: an HTTP 404 from another host is identified as an external party (by its content address), beside the QPU rights-holder and the tenant; the court rules in favour of the QPU licence where validatable via Payload, naming the Payload call that confirms it — never asserting it unvalidated.
- `legal-court-repo-audit`: The court decides only on an audited repo: every case names the repo-audit precondition — a quantum computation (verify the repo by content address, nanoseconds, beating classical recompute by magnitudes) sliced into nanosecond tasks, validatable via the audit tool — so no ruling stands on an unverified repo.
- `legal-plan`: Phase one on fixture texts and a three-door discovery: the claims gathered (licence, author, metadata, measured table, perspectives), the tree's security checks, and the plan — one forensics call per claim, the rights and security doors once, free by default.
