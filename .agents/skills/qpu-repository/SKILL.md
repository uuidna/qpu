## How a client uses it

The client gathers with git: the remote url, HEAD, the branch, package.json's version, worktree list, every branch with its upstream and head, every tag with its commit, and the concept DOI from .zenodo.json's related identifiers; calls repository for the requests; fetches them (GitHub needs no token for public reads); calls again with the answers; writes repository.json and the page; its leads join the next-leads docket. Offline, the local facts alone are written.
