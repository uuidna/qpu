# QPU · billing

Stripe per tenant: a licence bought at one tenant is an entry valid on the domains it names, every event idempotent and linked, every signature verified with the tenant's own secret, every refusal a lead

Generated by `payload { scripts: { skills: true } }` from the toolbox itself (2 tool(s), 9 example(s), 3 direction(s)); edit the toolbox, never this file.

## How a client uses it

Per tenant the author sets two wrangler secrets, STRIPE_SECRET_KEY_<TENANT> and STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET_<TENANT>, and registers https://<host>/docket/stripe as the webhook endpoint at Stripe. A client calls stripe plan and the Worker executes the plan with the tenant key, answering the session url. Stripe posts events to the tenant's endpoint; the Worker calls stripe webhook with the raw body, the Stripe-Signature header and the tenant secret, writes the entry it gets to the tenant docket (mirrors carry it to the others), and answers 200 on a duplicate. Any host answers stripe state for a subject at its domain from the entries it holds.

- `stripe-fusion`: Licensing by architecture: the quantum core computes on the classical world only through the fusion of coins into coils, and back the same way: the pure algebra stays a coin (free under attribution, the a432 default), every call that reaches classical data or services is a coil covered by the licence; every result carries its side, and the Worker refuses a declared commercial coil without a licence with the case the court computes; the two directions are the two loops of the double torus.
- `stripe-plan`: A checkout plan for a yearly commercial licence of acme on two domains, bought at tenant psg: the form Stripe takes, the metadata the webhook brings back, the tenant secret the Worker alone holds.
