## Stopping the System

The system runs infinitely (by topology). To pause:
- Press Ctrl+C to stop current wave
- System will auto-restart in 5 seconds (resilience loop)
- To stop permanently, kill the npm process

To prevent auto-restart:
- Edit `scripts/continuous-self-improvement.sh`
- Comment out final `exec "$0"` line
