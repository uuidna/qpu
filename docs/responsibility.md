# Responsibility

Google Quantum AI's statement on its technological and societal responsibilities, as its published PDF states it. The statement is documentation, not code.

- Google aims to be honest and realistic about the progress and limitations of quantum computing, and says many in the industry are exaggerating its current capabilities.
- A fault-tolerant quantum computer could break RSA by factoring large primes, which would compromise the integrity of electronic communication. The statement names this as a reason for collaboration across sectors and geographies.
- Post-quantum cryptography is the security community's response. NIST has announced a first set of post-quantum tools to be standardized, one of which, SPHINCS+, was developed with Google's contributions. Google says it is taking its own steps to keep Google Cloud, Android and Chrome secure.
- Google advises and collaborates with academic institutions, including UC Santa Barbara, UC Berkeley, MIT, Harvard, Oxford and the University of Toronto, and has released open-source tools for quantum computing algorithm and simulation software.
- Google's stated vision is that people will use classical computers as well as quantum computers to expand the boundaries of human knowledge and solve some of the world's most complex problems.
