# QPU full stack

The source of payloadcms.com (MIT) is the full stack, forked under site/: its 12 collections, 30 blocks, 5 globals and 10 fields kept. Applied: `src/blocks/Qpu/config.ts` (the qpu block, the shape every tools/call result carries in _meta.payload), registered in payload.config blocks and the pages layout; `src/components/blocks/Qpu/index.tsx` (the renderer: the page asks the Worker for the block and carries its widget), mapped in RenderBlocks; `src/payload.qpu.config.ts` (search and multi-tenant beside the site's four plugins, the database the RAID); `src/collections/Tenants.ts` (the multi-tenant plugin's own collection: the hosts that name the tenants). The catch-all makes prefixes useless: next.config rewrites any path that is a UUID to the Worker's page before the catch-all, and /mcp, /docket and /.well-known to the Worker. Deploy at Cloudflare beside this Worker; every step at whole uptime: configure { kind: 'migration' }.
