# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. Its pledge, its standards, its enforcement responsibilities and its enforcement guidelines apply in full to every space of the project: the repository, its issues and pull requests, the sites it serves and the MCP servers it runs.

## Reporting

Report conduct that breaks the Covenant to the author through [qpu.uuidna.com](https://qpu.uuidna.com). No address is kept in this repository. Every report is reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter are respected.

## What is computed, and what is not

The tree computes the mechanical part of conduct:

- **Roles:** what each role may do. A value a role may not choose is corrected to one it may, under role-based access control.
- **Rule breaks:** a request that breaks one of the tree's rules gets a case and a remedy from the court, recorded as a docket entry.

Whether a person broke this code, and what follows, is never computed. Each enforcement decision follows the Covenant's guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban. The author makes that decision as a human judgement about a human act.
