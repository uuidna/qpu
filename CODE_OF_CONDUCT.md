# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The QPU (Quantum Processing Unit) community is committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all participants, regardless of:

- Age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression
- Level of experience, education, socio-economic status
- Nationality, personal appearance, race, religion
- Sexual identity and orientation
- Or any other dimension of diversity

We are dedicated to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Giving credit and recognition to others' work
- Helping newer members learn and grow

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Deliberate spreading of misinformation
- Advocating for or encouraging harassment of any individual
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All community spaces (GitHub, issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Public and private interactions among community members
- Representing the project or community in any capacity

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

### Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. **Contact maintainers** at conduct@uuidna.org
2. **Provide details** of the incident with context
3. **Include evidence** if available (screenshots, links, etc.)
4. **Keep it confidential** - we will not share reports publicly

All reports will be:
- ✅ Reviewed promptly and fairly
- ✅ Kept confidential
- ✅ Investigated thoroughly
- ✅ Responded to within 7 days

### Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

1. **Warning** - First offense, clear communication of issue
2. **Temporary suspension** - 7-30 days, depending on severity
3. **Permanent removal** - For severe or repeated violations

## Appeal Process

If you believe enforcement action was unjust:

1. **Submit appeal** to conduct@uuidna.org within 14 days
2. **Include reasoning** for why you believe the action was unfair
3. **Provide new evidence** if available
4. **Receive decision** within 7 days

Appeals will be reviewed by a committee of at least 3 maintainers not involved in the original decision.

## Values We Uphold

### Respectful Discourse

- Disagree on ideas, not people
- Assume good intent until proven otherwise
- Engage in constructive dialogue
- Listen actively to understand different perspectives

### Inclusivity

- Welcome people from all backgrounds
- Make effort to include underrepresented voices
- Provide accommodations when requested
- Ensure accessibility in our interactions

### Integrity

- Be honest and transparent
- Take responsibility for mistakes
- Give credit where due
- Maintain confidentiality when requested

### Collaboration

- Work together toward shared goals
- Support each other's growth
- Celebrate successes together
- Help newer members learn and contribute
